The AI Growth Journal moved off a static mockup and onto a real Next.js site. Every research entry now follows a fixed structure (context, problem, hypothesis, research, implementation, results, lessons), case studies read like technical papers with client-verified metrics, and the whole thing ships with JSON-LD, a sitemap, and explicit crawl rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot.

Nothing about the writing changes — this was purely infrastructure, so the archive can actually hold hundreds of entries without turning into an unmaintainable pile of HTML.
